2 · Request a quote

Have these ready for an accurate price

✈
ModeAir, air consol, sea LCL or FCL, courier or road. Not sure? Pick the closest, we'll advise.
⇄
Route & termsOrigin and destination (city, port or airport) and the Incoterm, e.g. FOB or EXW.
📅
Ready dateWhen the cargo can be collected from your supplier.
$
Goods valueCommercial value and currency, for insurance and duties.
▦
CargoPieces, cartons, size per carton (L × W × H cm) and weight per carton.
⌂
AddressesYour supplier's collection address and the delivery address.
#
Your referenceYour PO or order number, so the quote and shipment carry it.
!
Special notesHazardous, stackable, container sizes (FCL), customs clearance needed.
Tip: add your supplier under Customer Party first (section 6), so their details are on file for the quote and the booking.

2 · Request a quote

Request a quote in four steps

  • 1
    How should it move?Go to Quotations › + Request Quote and pick the mode.
  • 2
    Route & termsOrigin, destination, Incoterm, ready date, commodity, your PO and the goods value.
  • 3
    CargoOne line per carton size. + Add line for more; the chargeable weight is worked out for you.
  • 4
    Addresses & sendCollection and delivery addresses, any notes, then Request a Quote.

4 · Ask for a revision

Need something changed?

  • 1
    Request a revisionOn the open quotation, click Request a revision.
  • 2
    Tick what to changeBetter price, route or mode, dates or transit time, cargo details, or other.
  • 3
    Add a target and a noteOptional target price incl. VAT, and anything that helps us, then Request revision.
Same quote number. The revised quotation comes back as "Revision 2" in the same place, and by email.

Quick answers

Good to know

How long does a quote take?Our team prices requests during business hours. You're emailed as soon as it's ready.
I'm not sure of the Incoterm or mode.Choose "Not sure" or the closest mode and say so in the notes; we'll advise the best option.
Full container (FCL)?Cargo lines are optional: put the container sizes in the notes, e.g. 2 × 40HC.
Can I change a quote after sending it?Yes: once it's priced, use Request a revision. Before that, message us on Chat.
Where's my accepted quote?Under Shipments, with the same number. Its timeline starts at Booked.
Does the portal work on my phone?Yes, in any phone browser. The menu folds away and everything stacks.
Can colleagues use it too?Yes, ask us for a login per person; each sees your company's quotes and shipments.
What do the dates on tracking mean?Ticked steps are confirmed with their date; "Expected" dates are the current plan and update as the shipment moves.
