Which milestones you update

PartnerYou setYou fill in
Origin agentBooked, Collected, Received (CFS / airline), Loaded, DepartedBooking details: carrier, shipping line, vessel or flight, voyage, MBL / MAWB, container and seal, ETD and ETA
TransporterCollected, Out for delivery, DeliveredWho signed for it and the delivery date, then upload the signed POD
Destination agentArrived, Discharged, Out for delivery, DeliveredArrival notification and POD
Clearing agentEntry lodged, Query raised, Customs cleared (Customs Files)Entry number, release note, customs entry, duty / VAT invoice
Running late? Use Report a delay on the shipment: say what happened and give the new ETA or delivery date. ExPac gets it as a priority task.

Quick answers

Good to know

Why don't I see a shipment?Only shipments ExPac has assigned to your company show. Ask us if one is missing.
Can I see the customer or our selling price?No. Your profile shows your leg of the shipment and your own rates only.
I entered a wrong date or number.Save the correct one. Every change is logged, and the latest value counts.
Who sees my messages?ExPac's ops team. Messages never go to the customer.
Can I delete an upload?No, uploads are kept for the record. Upload the right file and message us to ignore the wrong one.
Too many emails?Alerts are grouped: one email per round of changes, not one per change.
